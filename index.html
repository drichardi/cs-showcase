What's the DTE distinction?

Seven semesters of CS coursework. Start by sophomore year if you're aiming for it.

Do I have to take an AP?

No. Intro → Data Structures and Algorithms → Software Development Studio (K) reaches the capstone with no AP at all.

Can I double up?

Often. One AP plus Data Structures and Algorithms tends to leave students with a deeper skill set than two APs.
